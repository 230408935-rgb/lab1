About This Website

Our Purpose

This website is the home of the Starlight Astronomy Club, a fictional community group that makes astronomy friendly and accessible to everyone. Here you can read about upcoming events, browse our programs and contact our volunteers.

The club was founded on September 12, 2015 by a small group of friends with one telescope and a lot of curiosity.

Somewhere, something incredible is waiting to be known.

— Often attributed to Carl Sagan

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own telescope?

No. The club lends telescopes and binoculars to all attendees during events.

Is membership free?

Attending events is free. Optional membership supports equipment and maintenance.

Did You Know?

Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth, while light from the nearest other star takes over 4 years.

Water boils at about 100°C, but H2O ice exists on the Moon's poles.
